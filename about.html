About Kali Travels

Travel made simple, memories made forever.

At Kali Travels, we believe every journey should be seamless, exciting, and unforgettable. We help travelers discover amazing destinations while taking care of the planning, so you can focus on enjoying every moment.

Whether you're traveling for leisure, business, or adventure, our goal is to provide personalized travel experiences, reliable support, and carefully selected destinations that match your travel dreams.

Your journey starts with Kali Travels.

Our origin

Fifteen years of "just one more stamp in the passport"

Founders Ana Reyes and Tom Okafor met guiding the same overland route through East Africa. Both kept hearing the same complaint from travelers: agencies sold the destination, then handed off the details to someone who'd never been there.

So we built the opposite — a small agency where every planner has personally traveled every route they sell, and a trip isn't "finished" until the planner would send their own family on it.

2011

Two guides, one shared spreadsheet

Kali Travels starts as a side project booking trips for friends of friends.

2015

First five full-time planners

Each hired for a region they'd lived in, not just visited.

2019

The "one planner" rule

We formalize what became our biggest differentiator: one dedicated planner per trip, start to finish.

2026

4,300+ trips designed

Still a small team, still turning down destinations we don't know firsthand.

What we won't compromise on

Our four rules

01

Planners must have been there

No one sells a destination they haven't personally traveled in the last two years.

02

One planner per trip

You never get bounced between reps who don't know your itinerary.

03

Fewer destinations, deeper knowledge

We'd rather know 18 places well than list 200 we've only read about.

04

Support doesn't end at booking

Your planner is reachable for the entire length of your trip, not just up to departure.
