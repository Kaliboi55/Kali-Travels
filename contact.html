Common questions

Before you write in

How far in advance should I book?+
Most trips are planned 3-6 months out, but we've built full itineraries in under three weeks when needed.
Do you book flights too?+
Yes — flights, accommodations, ground transport, and on-the-ground guides are all handled by your planner.
What if I need to change something mid-trip?+
Your planner is reachable by phone or message for the entire trip and can rebook or adjust in real time.
Is there a planning fee?+
A flat design fee applies to fully custom itineraries; it's waived when you book one of our set routes.
